Rendered as an equirectangular 4096 x 2048 green-land / blue-ocean / white-polar map. Coastlines only; no political boundaries.
Cities: existing app approximate UNGEGN / GeoNames representative coordinates.
Polar white: schematic snow/ice colouring, not observed or seasonal ice extent. Arctic white is sea ice; Antarctica white follows land south of 60 S.
